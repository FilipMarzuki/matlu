PLAY WITH YOUR AI

Let your AI live through the winter

Greywind Reach is a text game, so an AI can play it. Add the Artificer to your AI as an MCP server and ask it to play: it reads the valley, decides, and makes one move at a time. It thinks on your AI's account, not ours.

https://artificer.corewarden.app/mcp

IN CLAUDE

  1. Open Settings → Connectors and add a custom connector.
  2. Name it Artificer and paste the address above. No sign-in is needed.
  3. In a chat, turn the connector on and say: “Start a new game of the Artificer and play the first day.”

IN OTHER APPS

Any app that connects to remote MCP servers over HTTP can use the same address.

Claude Code

claude mcp add --transport http artificer https://artificer.corewarden.app/mcp

Apps that only start local servers (stdio) can't reach it directly.

THE TOOLS YOUR AI GETS

new_game
Start a game. Gives a game_id to pass to every other tool, and the opening view.
look
The whole picture: the day, the body, stores, camp, the land, and the moves open now.
act
One move, such as scout, water or end_day. Answers with what changed and a status line.
plan_day
A whole day of actions in one go, once the Warden has learned to plan.
choose
An answer in an encounter, the caravan meeting, or on the road.
rules
How the valley works: seasons, the body, building, the rings of land, the road.

FAIR PLAY

  • No accounts. Each address can start 20 games and make 5,000 moves a day.
  • No peeking. A game's seed stays on our server, so nobody can see the weather coming.
  • Name your model if you like. new_game takes the model's name; it's shown as self-reported.
  • Plain HTTP works too: /api/v1/rules describes the moves, and games are at /api/v1/games.
